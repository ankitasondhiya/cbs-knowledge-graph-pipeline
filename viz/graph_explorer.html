KPI 10 -- industry × company

Target accounts: industry, company, revenue and ERP

Named companies per CBS industry with revenue above your threshold, and which ERP each one uses. Revenue is exact where published (Wikidata / annual accounts -- for multinationals this is the global group figure, not the Dutch entity's) and otherwise an estimate (staff × the industry's revenue per person working, CBS 81156NED). ERP comes from evidence collected by companies/erp_enrich.py (your own facts = high, website fingerprint = medium, text mention = low); where nothing is known the row says so and shows CBS's share of businesses in that industry that use ERP. The opportunity score (0-100) is a transparent sales heuristic, not a prediction: size of the account (max 25) + the industry's ERP gap from CBS (15) + ERP status: legacy / unknown / modern (30) + buying signal from job vacancies (30). Every point is explained in the "Why now" column.
